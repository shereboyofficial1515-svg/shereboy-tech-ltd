FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTION

Find answers to frequently asked questions about our product and services

Why does my business need a website?

A website acts as your business’s digital home where customers can find you anytime, anywhere.

Unlike a physical shop that closes at night, a website works for you 24 hours a day. It allows people to learn about your services, see your products, read reviews, and contact you easily.

Can a website help my business get more customers?

Yes. A website helps you reach people beyond your physical location. With search engines like Google, customers can find your business when they search for products or services you offer. For example, if someone searches for “best graphic designer near me” or “affordable phone repair,” your website can appear in the search results if properly optimized

This means people who have never heard about your business can discover you online.

Is a website better than relying only on social media?

Social media is useful, but it should not be your only online presence. Platforms like Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok are controlled by the companies that own them Their algorithms can change anytime, which may limit how many people see your content.

With a website, you fully control your content, design, and information. You can showcase your services professionally, collect customer inquiries and even sell products directly without depending entirely on social media platforms.

Does a website make my business look more professional?

Yes. A well-designed website shows customers that your business is serious and trustworthy. It allows you to present your brand professionally with clear information about your services, pricing, contact details, and customer testimonials.

Many customers judge a business based on its online presence. If your competitors have professional websites and you do not, potential clients may choose them instead. A good website creates a strong first impression and helps build long-term credibility.

How much does it cost to create a website?

The cost of creating a website can vary widely depending on your needs. A simple informational website with a few pages may cost less, while a complex e-commerce site with custom features will be more expensive.

At SHEREBOY TECH LTD, we offer affordable web development packages that can fit different budgets. We work closely with you to understand your requirements and provide a solution that delivers value for your investment.

How long does it take to build a website?

The time required to build a website depends on its complexity and the features you need. A basic website can be completed in a few weeks, while a more complex site may take several months.

At SHEREBOY TECH LTD, we provide clear timelines and keep you updated throughout the development process. We strive to deliver high-quality websites efficiently without compromising on quality.
